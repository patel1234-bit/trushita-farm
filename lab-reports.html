Lab Testing & Purity FAQs

Why is there slight natural sediment at the bottom of pure oil? ▼

Slight natural cloudiness or fine seed particles at the base are the signature of authentic unrefined oil. Unlike factory oils treated with chemical gums and centrifugal stripping, our 4-layer cotton cloth filtration preserves healthy micronutrients.

What does "Zero Hexane Residue" in the NABL report mean? ▼

Industrial oil refineries use hexane (a petroleum-derived solvent) to maximize oil yield from seed meal. Our wooden kolhus press purely mechanically without a single drop of solvent, confirmed as 0.00 ppm on every NABL certificate.

How long does Trushita wood-pressed oil stay fresh? ▼

Because natural Vitamin E (tocopherols) remains intact in wood-pressed oils, they naturally resist oxidation for 9 to 12 months when stored in a cool, dark pantry away from direct sunlight.
